## 2. Two kinds of personal data

**(a) Account data — we are the data user.** This is personal data about the people who sign up for and use the Platform. We decide how this data is used.

**(b) Customer data — the customer company is the data user.** Our customers put personal data about their own staff, customers, suppliers, tenants and visitors into the Apps. For that data:
- the customer company decides how it is used, and is responsible for it under the PDPA;
- we only store and process it on the customer's behalf, to provide the Platform.

If your data is in a customer's records, please contact that company first. We will help them answer your request.

The rest of this notice is about account data.

## 3. What we collect

- **Identity and contact:** name, display name, email address, phone number, job title, user ID.
- **Company details:** company name, registration and SST numbers, address, logo, and who the company's administrators are.
- **Login and security:** password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), sign-in times, devices and sessions, IP address, and a record of important actions taken.
- **Billing:** the plans bought, amounts, dates, receipt numbers, and payment references from Billplz. Card and online-banking details are entered on Billplz's pages. We never receive or store them.
- **Support:** messages and feedback you send us, and records of support visits.

**What you must give:** your name, email address and password are needed to create an account. Company details and payment are needed to subscribe. Without them we cannot provide the service. Everything else is optional.

## 4. Why we use it

- To create and run your account, sign you in, and keep the Platform secure (including detecting misuse).
- To provide the Apps your company subscribes to.
- To take payment and issue receipts.
- To send service emails: email verification, password resets, trial and renewal reminders, receipts, and important notices.
- To give support.
- To meet our legal, tax and accounting duties.
- To improve the Platform, using usage information in a way that does not single you out.

We do not sell personal data. We do not send marketing emails unless you agree to them, and you can say no at any time.

## 5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with:
- **Service providers** that run parts of the Platform for us, under contract and only for that purpose:
  - hosting and servers (Render);
  - database and file storage (Supabase);
  - email delivery (Brevo);
  - payments (Billplz);
- **Your own company's administrators**, who can see the users in their company and what they do in the Apps.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.
- **A buyer or successor** of our business, under the same protections, if the business is sold or reorganised.

## 6. Transfer outside Malaysia

Our service providers keep data on servers outside Malaysia, in Singapore, Australia (Sydney) and Japan (Tokyo). We transfer data only as allowed by the PDPA and take steps so that it is protected to a standard similar to the PDPA.

## 7. How long we keep it

- **Account data:** kept while your account is active. When an account is closed, the data is kept for 2 years for security and dispute records, then deleted or made anonymous.
- **Billing and receipt records:** kept for 7 years, as required for tax and company records.
- **Customer data:** kept as described in our Terms of Service (section 8).

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with:
- encrypted connections (HTTPS);
- hashed passwords;
- a separate space for each company's data;
- access limited to authorised staff, with every support visit time-limited and recorded;
- regular backups.

No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will notify the Personal Data Protection Commissioner and the affected people as the PDPA requires.

## 9. Your rights

You may:
- ask to **access** the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy;
- ask us to **correct** data that is wrong, incomplete or out of date — most of it you can change yourself under My Profile;
- **withdraw consent** or ask us to **limit** how we use your data (if you do, we may not be able to keep providing the service);
- ask us to **transfer** your data to you or another provider, where the PDPA allows;
- **complain** to us, or to the Personal Data Protection Commissioner.

Send requests to lim@fostercircle.com. We will reply within 21 days. A fee allowed by the PDPA may apply to access requests.
